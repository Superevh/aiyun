About Me

Simple, practical,
human AI.

I'm Evan — an AI thinker, maker, and storyteller. I help people understand how AI can make their work easier, their ideas bigger, and their lives a little more interesting.

Over the years I've worked across engineering, delivery, transformation, and product. All of that experience now converges into one mission: helping people use AI in a simple, practical, human way.

I build small products, run experiments, and explore how AI reshapes strategy, ways of working, creativity, and everyday life. I treat this space as both a craft and a playground.
